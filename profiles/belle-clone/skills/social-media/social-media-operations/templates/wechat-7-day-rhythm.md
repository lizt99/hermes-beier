# WeChat 7-Day Rhythm

Derived from the archived `wechat-debt-support-group-ops` skill.

## Suggested cadence
- Day 1: welcome + group purpose + rule reinforcement
- Day 2: emotional check-in topic
- Day 3: practical checklist / myth-busting topic
- Day 4: anonymized case discussion
- Day 5: need-tag review and targeted prompts
- Day 6: guided Q&A / service-window appearance
- Day 7: weekly summary + next-step invitation
